Welcome to FraudLens
Three steps, about one minute

1. Pin FraudLens to the toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon 🧩 at the top right of Chrome, then the pin 📌 next to FraudLens Guard. Clicking the FraudLens icon opens its panel.

2. Connect to your FraudLens server

Paste the address of your server on Render (it looks like https://fraudlens-xxxx.onrender.com) and click Connect. Chrome then asks to let FraudLens talk to that address: click Allow.

3. Try it

Open the practice pages: a scam inbox, a fake bank page with Pause-Before-Pay, and an attack page.

Later, to protect Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp Web or every website: FraudLens panel → Settings & privacy.
